Krona is a financial manager and expense tracker app.

Highlights:
- Track your expenses across multiple accounts
- Create an infinite number of categories with arbitrary level of nesting
- Split payments with friends and track how much you owe them
- Import your expenses history from your favourite apps - Zen Money and Cashew
- See your total balance at a glance
- Track monthly statistics with colorful charts

Soon to come:
- Icons for categories
- More types of statistics
- Support for cryptocurrencies
- More import options
- Search and filter for transactions
